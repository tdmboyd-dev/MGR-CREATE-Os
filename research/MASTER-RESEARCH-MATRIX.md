## Initial architecture consequences already strong enough to preserve
- Provider/model independence is mandatory.
- Research evidence must be first-class data, not prose only.
- Asset/version/review lineage must be first-class.
- Durable workflows need persisted state, retries, idempotency and compensation.
- Creation and permission to act are separate.
- Brand/identity/continuity context must travel with work.
- Verification must inspect the real output.
- Feedback and analytics feed IMPROVE rather than ending at publish.
- MCP is one interface option; core domain logic must not depend on MCP.
- Legacy MGR Agents concepts remain untrusted until traced and proven.
