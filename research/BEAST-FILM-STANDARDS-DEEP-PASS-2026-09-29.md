# BEAST Film / VFX Standards Deep Pass — 2026-09-29

Creation OS should learn from mature film/VFX interchange without letting any one standard own MGR's internal domain model.

OpenUSD: ADAPT for scene composition, layers, references, variants and persistent worlds.
OpenTimelineIO: ADOPT AS ADAPTER for clips/timing/tracks/transitions/markers while media stays external.
OpenColorIO + ACES: ADOPT/ADAPT for explicit production color management.
MaterialX: ADAPT for portable material/look-development graphs.
OpenEXR: ADOPT for professional scene-linear HDR/multichannel interchange.
OpenVDB: STUDY/ADAPT when volumetric smoke/fire/cloud/fog workflows justify it.
OpenCue: STUDY; adopt only when MGR actually reaches heterogeneous render-farm scale.

MGR-native contracts remain SceneGraph, WorldVersion, CameraRig, LightRig, MaterialGraph, VolumeAsset, EditorialTimeline, RenderJob, ArtifactVersion and ProvenanceRecord.

Interchange standards make us portable; they do not choose product behavior or providers.
