# Document Factory
Mission: semantic content → structured branded validated documents.
Study Pandoc, Quarto, reference-document/template systems and document automation.
Extract canonical document model, sections, citations, tables/figures, themes, reusable blocks, page rules, PDF/DOCX/EPUB/HTML/Markdown export, metadata, TOC, accessibility, localization, versioning and template population.
Verify by opening/parsing every output and checking pagination, overflow, images, links, TOC, styles and citations.
