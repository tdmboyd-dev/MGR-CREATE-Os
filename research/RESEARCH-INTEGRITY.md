# Research Integrity Correction

## Why this exists
The earlier Wave 1/Wave 2 files were architectural synthesis passes, not the full BEAST University deep-research standard promised by this repository. They were incorrectly labeled RESEARCHED.

BEAST status has been corrected:
- SYNTHESIZED = internal read + architecture hypothesis.
- BEAST-RESEARCHED = multi-source external research across appropriate universities, source/evidence ledger, competing approaches, failure modes, licensing/security/cost, native-contract consequences, and verification plan.
- SPECIFIED/IMPLEMENTED/TESTED/VERIFIED remain later states.
