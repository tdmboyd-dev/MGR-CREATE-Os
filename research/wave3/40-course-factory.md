# 40 Course Factory — BEAST RESEARCH WAVE 3
Universities: open LMS architectures, instructional design, SCORM/xAPI concepts, adaptive learning, assessment generation, accessibility, learning analytics.
Architecture: SourceEvidence→Learner/Audience→Objectives→Prerequisite graph→Syllabus→Lessons→Practice→Assessment→Remediation→Delivery→Learning analytics.
Required: objective alignment, examples/exercises, quiz item bank, rubrics, answer security, spaced review, instructor/learner assets, media derivatives, LMS exports, certificates with evidence.
Failure: content dump instead of pedagogy, bad progression, weak distractors, unsupported facts, inaccessible material, assessment not aligned to objective.
Verification: objective↔lesson↔assessment traceability, key validation, navigation/accessibility and package tests.
