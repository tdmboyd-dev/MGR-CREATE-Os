# 39 Campaign Factory — BEAST RESEARCH WAVE 3
Universities: Adobe scaled creative production; Canva brand governance; CRM lifecycle systems; ad/email/social platform structures; experimentation/analytics.
Architecture: Research→Audience/Problem→CampaignBrief→Message hierarchy→Channel plan→Content matrix→Variants→Approval→Distribution→Attribution→Experiment→Improve.
Creation and permission are separate: generating ads never authorizes spend or publishing.
Required: claims/evidence, segments, creative variants, calendars, UTM/tracking, channel adapters, spend policy, fatigue, attribution limits, experiments.
Failure: unsupported claims, audience leakage, broken links/tracking, duplicate sends, overspend, attribution overclaim, creative fatigue.
Verification: previews + destination/tracking tests + platform ID reconciliation + performance events.
