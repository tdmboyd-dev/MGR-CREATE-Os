# 48 Cross-Factory Objective Orchestrator — BEAST RESEARCH WAVE 3
Mission: make “build me a tax-preparer education business” a governed objective, not twelve manual prompts.
Universities: hierarchical task planning, project/program management, durable workflows, multi-agent orchestration, dependency/resource scheduling, production pipelines.
Architecture: Objective→Outcome/constraints→Research→Work Breakdown Graph→Factory selection→Dependency DAG→budget/risk/approval plan→parallel factory subprojects→artifact contracts→integration gates→verification→launch→measurement/improvement.
Core object: ObjectivePlan with outcomes, acceptance criteria, workstreams, dependencies, owners/agents, factories, budgets, risks, approvals, artifacts and evidence.
Key rule: factories remain independently executable. Objective Orchestrator coordinates them through contracts/events; it does not become one giant god-agent.
Failure: circular dependencies, conflicting factory outputs, budget runaway, context contamination, premature downstream work, approval deadlock, objective drift.
Verification: simulate and then execute reference objectives; every final deliverable traces to objective acceptance criterion and factory evidence.
