# BEAST Dataset / Evaluation University — 2026-09-28

## Dataset is not a download
For every dataset: exact version/hash, source, license, underlying-asset rights, commercial use, redistribution, biometric/voice/PII risk, consent, geography, demographic/coverage analysis, annotation schema, known bias, duplicates, train/eval leakage, storage policy and intended use.

## Lifecycle
DatasetCandidate -> RightsReview -> Ingest -> Normalize -> Deduplicate -> QualityReport -> DatasetSnapshot -> Train/Adapt -> EvaluationSuite -> PromotionDecision -> Monitoring -> Rollback/Retire.

## Media/identity QA
Resolution/corruption; perceptual duplicates; subject count; face/body identity consistency; pose/view/expression diversity; lighting/background/wardrobe/prop leakage; caption accuracy; watermark/text contamination; unsafe content; synthetic/real provenance.

## Research leads requiring license-specific treatment
CameraBench, MovieShots, MultiCamVideo, CineScene, DataDoP, RealEstate10K/WebVid-derived camera corpora, SA-V, MAdVerse, Pitt Image Ads, AdsTrace. A paper or dataset card proves research relevance, not commercial training permission.

## MGR-owned datasets
The highest-value commercial corpus should be permissioned first-party evidence: iKickItz owner-approved character references and verified renders; MGR campaign creative/outcomes; Create Loco screenshot/reference/build/repair pairs; provider quality/cost/failure observations; explicitly consented voice/identity assets.

## Eval rule
Generation and verification are separate. Every capability defines measurable acceptance: identity, mask IoU/track consistency, edit locality, text accuracy, camera-motion match, temporal stability, lip-sync alignment, audio intelligibility, provenance, rights, format and cost.
