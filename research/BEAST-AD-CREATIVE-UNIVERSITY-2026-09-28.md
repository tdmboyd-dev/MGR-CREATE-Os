# BEAST Advertising / Creative University — First Deep Pass

## Core correction
A “Creative DNA Extractor” is not one classifier. It is a multimodal analysis graph.

## Input analyzers
Video -> shot boundaries, camera, subject/object tracks, OCR/captions, speech, speaker/voice, music/SFX, visual rhythm, transitions, product visibility, CTA timing.
Static ad -> layout, hierarchy, OCR, product/logo placement, color, faces, saliency, offer/proof/CTA, format.
URL -> product/service facts, price/offer, media, reviews/social proof, claims, brand assets, audience clues and landing-page conversion structure.
Campaign -> channel, objective, audience, spend, creative lineage, impressions/clicks/conversions/revenue and time series.

## Semantic creative schema
Hook, problem, desire, audience, angle, promise, mechanism, demonstration, proof, objection, offer, urgency, CTA, brand reveal, product reveal, creator/avatar, tone, emotion, shot scale, camera motion, visual tempo, caption style, music energy and ending.

## Performance truth
Do not train a “winning ad predictor” from aesthetics alone. Performance is confounded by targeting, placement, budget, auction, frequency, season, landing page, offer and brand strength. Store creative features and delivery context together. Use post-launch causal/experimental evidence where possible; prediction confidence must be calibrated.

## Standards and channel research
- IAB New Ad Portfolio: aspect-ratio based responsive display formats and LEAN principles.
- IAB HTML5: packaging/performance guidance for interactive ads.
- IAB video/CTV: file, ratio, playback and emerging pause/menu/screensaver formats.
- Google Ads API: campaigns, budgets, asset groups, assets, statuses and performance fields. Create campaigns paused by default in our adapter until approval.
- Meta/TikTok/AppLovin connectors require separate official-API/permission/policy dossiers before implementation.

## Dataset leads
- MAdVerse: >50K multilingual ads and hierarchy; CC BY-NC plus underlying-image rights warning -> research/evaluation only by default.
- Pitt Image Ads: topics, sentiments and visual rhetoric -> research lead, rights review.
- AdsTrace: second-by-second CTR/engagement/ROI/CVR research lead -> full license/source audit required.
- Criteo click/attribution datasets: useful for attribution/ranking mechanics, not visual-creative truth.
- MGR first-party campaign outcomes should become the primary commercial learning corpus with tenant/consent/privacy controls.

## Native MGR systems
ProductKnowledgeObject, CreativeDNA, CreativeVariant, CampaignPlan, ChannelAdapter, PerformanceObservation, Experiment, AttributionEvidence, CreativeFatigueSignal, Recommendation, ApprovalReceipt.

## Safety/rights
Do not clone copyrighted assets. Extract abstract structure and measurable properties. Preserve source/provenance. Ads with regulated claims need domain policy packs. Publishing/spend always passes Approval Gateway according to tenant policy.
