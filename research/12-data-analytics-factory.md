# Data / Analytics Factory
Mission: events/operational data → trustworthy metrics, experiments, alerts, diagnosis and improvement.
Study PostHog and warehouse/BI/event-schema/observability patterns.
Extract event taxonomy, identity/session model, ingestion, quality, semantic metrics, funnels/cohorts/retention/paths/attribution, dashboards, experiments/flags, replay links, anomaly alerts, cost telemetry, factory KPIs and UCT/workflow/run backlinks.
Research vanity metrics, duplicate events, definition drift, survivorship bias, attribution failure, bots/internal traffic, missing denominators, experiment peeking and correlation-as-causation.
