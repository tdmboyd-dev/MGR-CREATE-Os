# Automation Factory
Mission: inspectable durable recoverable automations and agent workflows.
Study n8n, Temporal, Activepieces and LangGraph.
Extract typed triggers/actions, graph IR, idempotency, durable state, retries/backoff, timeout/cancel, compensation/rollback, human gates, secret refs, sandboxing, connectors, schedules/events/webhooks, subflows, versioning, dry-run/replay, logs, MCP/API exposure and cost/rate limits.
Verify duplicate events, outages, timeouts, partial failure, restart recovery, bad credentials, rate limits, approval pause/resume, rollback and side-effect reconciliation.
