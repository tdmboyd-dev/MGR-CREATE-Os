# Canonical State Machines

CreationRun: QUEUED→PLANNING→RUNNING→VERIFYING→COMPLETED. Side states WAITING_APPROVAL/WAITING_EVENT/WAITING_RESOURCE return to RUNNING. Failure may RETRY or COMPENSATE; terminal FAILED/CANCELLED/COMPENSATED/COMPENSATION_FAILED.

StageRun: PENDING→READY→RUNNING→SUCCEEDED. RUNNING may WAIT_*; FAILED may RETRY_SCHEDULED→READY if policy permits. CANCELLED terminal.

ApprovalRequest: DRAFT→PENDING→APPROVED|REJECTED|EXPIRED|REVOKED. APPROVED becomes CONSUMED after exact action executes; changed digest requires new request.

AssetVersion: CREATED→VALIDATING→VALID|INVALID; valid version may IN_REVIEW→APPROVED|CHANGES_REQUESTED|REJECTED. Version content immutable.

Defect: OPEN→ROOT_CAUSE_INVESTIGATING→ROOT_CAUSE_PROVEN→REPAIRING→VERIFYING→RESOLVED; may BLOCKED/ESCALATED. Reopen creates new evidence event.

TrainingRun: QUEUED→PREPARING→RUNNING→EVALUATING→CANDIDATE|FAILED. Promotion separate: CANDIDATE→APPROVAL_PENDING→PROMOTED; rollback moves alias.

ResearchRun: PLANNING→GATHERING→REVIEWING→GAP_RESEARCH→SYNTHESIZING→VERIFYING→COMPLETE; can loop REVIEWING↔GAP_RESEARCH within bounded budget.

TemplatePackage: DRAFT→VALIDATING→VALIDATED→PUBLISHED→DEPRECATED|REVOKED.
